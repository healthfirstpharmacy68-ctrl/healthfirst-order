Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you deliver to?

Anand Nagar, Dahisar East & West, Borivali East & West, Kandivali, Mira Road, I C Colony, Ganpat Patil Nagar, Gorai and Kanderpada. Your order is delivered soon after we confirm it.

Is there a delivery charge?

Delivery is free on orders of ₹200 and above, and always free in Anand Nagar. Other areas pay a ₹20 delivery charge on orders below ₹200.

How do I order prescription medicines?

Add them to your cart and place the order. Our pharmacist will call you and ask you to share the prescription on WhatsApp before packing.

How can I pay?

Pay on delivery by cash, UPI or card (we bring a card machine to your door), or pay now with any UPI app such as Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm. You can also pick up from our store and pay there.

How do I track my order?

Open My orders at the top of the page to see the live status of your order.
